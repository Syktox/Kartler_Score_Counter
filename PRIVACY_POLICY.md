# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Kartler is a scorekeeping app for counters and card games. This policy explains what data the app stores, how optional store donations work, and how external links are handled.

## Data Stored By The App

Kartler stores game state locally on your device with `shared_preferences`. This can include:

- Counter names and values
- Watten game names, sides, scores, and selected game state
- Mulatschak player names, scores, history, and reset settings
- App settings such as selected mode and theme

This data stays on your device. Kartler does not send these saved scores, names, settings, or history entries to the developer or to an external server.

## External Links

Kartler may open external links, such as bug report links, with `url_launcher`. When you open an external link, your browser or the target app handles that website or service. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Store Donations

Kartler may offer optional donations through the store billing system. Payment processing is handled by the app store, such as Google Play on Android. The store may provide purchase status updates to Kartler so the transaction can be completed. Donations do not unlock app features.

## Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

Kartler does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data Sharing

Kartler does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored game data.

## Data Deletion

You can delete stored game data by using the app controls to remove counters, games, or players where available. You can also clear all app data through your device settings or uninstall the app.

## Children's Privacy

Kartler is a general-purpose scorekeeping app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data handling changes. Updates will be published with the app documentation or release notes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository.
